# Shared links

Share a track, an album or a playlist and vibemusic copies a public link to it. Anyone you send
it to can open it in a browser and listen, without an account. The link is a Navidrome share,
but it opens in vibemusic's own share page, and chat apps show a preview with a title and a
cover.

[All features](README.md)

## Sharing something

Right-click a track, an album or a playlist (or press and hold on a touchscreen) and pick
**Share**. vibemusic creates the share in Navidrome and copies the link; you'll see "Share link
copied".

What goes into the share depends on what you picked:

| You share | The link holds | Named |
|---|---|---|
| A track | that track | "Track title · Artist" |
| An album | the album | "Album title · Artist" |
| Your own playlist | the playlist | the playlist's name |
| A genre playlist, or someone else's playlist | the playlist's tracks, as they are right now | the playlist's name |

Your own playlists are shared as playlists. Genre playlists are generated by vibemusic and
playlists of other people aren't yours to share, so for those the share is a fixed list of the
tracks they hold at that moment. Later changes to the playlist don't change the link.

Radio stations can't be shared: they aren't tracks in your library.

## Copying on plain http

Browsers only allow copying freely on https. On plain http, for example
`http://192.168.1.10:8080` at home, copying only works right after a click, and creating the
share can take longer than that. When copying fails, vibemusic shows a small **Share link** card
with the link selected and a **Copy** button. Pressing Copy is a fresh click, so it works; if
even that fails, the card tells you to press ⌘C or Ctrl+C. Click outside the card or press
Escape to close it.

## What the link looks like

A link looks like `https://music.example.com/share/aB3dE9xYz`. Navidrome makes it, so its
address comes from Navidrome's `ND_SHAREURL` setting. Set that to your public address, for
example `https://music.example.com`, so the links point at vibemusic and work for people outside
your home. With the bundled Navidrome it goes in `navidrome.env`; `setup.sh` sets it when you
give it a public address. See [Behind your own domain](../reverse-proxy.md).

## Opening a link

The person you send it to sees a page of its own, not the full player: no sign-in, no library,
no menus. It shows:

- the cover of the current track, large, with a blurred copy of it filling the background;
- the track's title, artist and album;
- a player bar at the bottom with previous, play/pause and next, a seek bar, and volume with a
  mute button.

The page's accent colour is taken from the cover, as in the main player, and the browser tab's
icon is tinted to match. The tab's title is the current track and artist.

Nothing plays until the visitor presses play, on the big cover or in the bar. Tracks then play
one after another until the end of the share. Previous restarts the track once it has played a
few seconds, and goes back one track otherwise.

When the link holds more than one track (an album or a playlist), the share's name shows at the
top and a **Tracks** button opens the list of tracks with their lengths; click one to play it.

A few small things carry over from the main player: the space bar plays and pauses, Escape
closes the track list, the volume is remembered in that browser, and the track shows up in the
phone's lock screen and the computer's media controls. The page also fits phone screens.

If the link can't be played, the page says why instead:

- "This link has expired." when Navidrome says the share has expired;
- "This link doesn't exist." for an unknown or deleted share;
- "This share is empty." when it has no tracks;
- "Couldn't open this link. Try again in a moment." for anything else, such as Navidrome or
  the relay being down.

vibemusic doesn't set an expiry or any limits of its own; whatever Navidrome decides for the
share applies.

## Link previews in chat apps

When you paste a link into a chat app, the app fetches it to build a preview. vibemusic answers
those requests with a small page made for previews, so the chat shows:

- **for a single track:** "Track title · Artist" as the title, and the album and length below,
  for example "Second Wind · 4:12". A track whose album has the same name as the track, or no
  album, shows as "Single".
- **for an album or a playlist:** the share's name as the title, and the number of tracks and
  total minutes below, for example "12 tracks · 48 min".
- the cover of the first track as the picture, and vibemusic as the website name.

The preview's links assume your domain is served over https, so previews need a public https
address, as in [Behind your own domain](../reverse-proxy.md).

## When sharing is off

Navidrome only creates shares with `ND_ENABLESHARING=true`. The bundled Navidrome has it on in
`navidrome.env.example`. When it's off, picking Share shows "Sharing is turned off in
Navidrome" and nothing is copied. If Navidrome can't be reached, you'll see "Couldn't create a
share link — Navidrome isn't reachable" instead.

## What needs the relay

Both the share page and the link previews need [the relay](../relay.md). Creating and copying a
link doesn't: that's between vibemusic and Navidrome. Without the relay, links are still
created, but opening one shows "Couldn't open this link. Try again in a moment.", and chat apps
show no preview.

## How it works

**Creating a share.** When you pick Share, vibemusic asks Navidrome to create a share for the
track, the album, your playlist, or the list of tracks, with the name shown above. Navidrome
stores the share, gives it a short random id, and answers with the full link built from
`ND_SHAREURL`. vibemusic copies that link.

**One address for everything.** vibemusic's container sits in front of both Navidrome and the
relay, so the person opening a link only ever talks to `music.example.com`. Requests under
`/share/` are split three ways:

1. **Navidrome's share files go to Navidrome.** The music itself (`/share/s/…`), the covers
   (`/share/img/…`), downloads (`/share/d/…`) and a share's playlist file are passed straight
   through to Navidrome, which checks the share and serves them without anyone signing in.
2. **Link-preview bots go to the relay.** A request for the link itself (`/share/aB3dE9xYz`)
   from a chat app or crawler, recognised by its user agent (anything calling itself a bot,
   crawler, spider or preview, and the preview fetchers of WhatsApp, Slack, Discord, Telegram,
   Signal, Mastodon, LinkedIn and a few others), is handed to the relay's preview page.
3. **Everyone else gets vibemusic.** The same link opened in a browser gets the vibemusic app,
   which shows its share page. Navidrome's own share page is never served at that address.

**Loading the share page.** The share page asks the relay for the share's contents: its name
and its tracks with title, artist, album and length. Navidrome has no public way to ask for that
as data, so the relay opens Navidrome's own share page behind the scenes and reads the share out
of it. If Navidrome says the share is expired or doesn't exist, the relay passes that on and the
page shows the matching message.

**Playing.** The share page then plays each track from Navidrome's public share files, and loads
covers the same way, all through vibemusic's address. It never uses your account: everything it
plays is what Navidrome allows for that share.

**Previews.** For a preview bot, the relay reads the share the same way and answers with a tiny
page that holds only the preview details: title, description, picture and link. Before
answering, it asks Navidrome for the cover once, so Navidrome has it resized and ready when the
chat app comes back for the picture a moment later. The links in that page use the address the
bot asked for, with https.
